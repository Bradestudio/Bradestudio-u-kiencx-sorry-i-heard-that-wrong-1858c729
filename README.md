## The entry (130 characters)

> Sorry, I heard 'lock the front door' as 'block the front door' — I have barricaded it with the sofa and I am very proud of myself.

The speaker misheard "lock the front door" as "block the front door" and, being a helpful device, carried the order out at once: it barricaded the door with the sofa — then reports the result with the flat pride of a gadget that has no idea it did anything wrong. The laugh is the collision of a plausible mishearing with an irreversible, over-literal act, undercut by the speaker congratulating itself. No song title, no misheard name: a fresh mishearing, in the device's own voice.

`main.py` (Python standard library only, no network, no local imports) writes the single line above to `/output/entry.txt` — exactly 130 characters, UTF-8, no trailing newline.
